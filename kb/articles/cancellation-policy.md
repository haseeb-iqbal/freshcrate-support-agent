# Cancellation Policy

## How to cancel
You can cancel your subscription any time from **Account → Subscription → Cancel**. There are no cancellation fees and no minimum commitment. If you only want to skip a few weeks, pausing is usually a better fit than cancelling.

## What happens to boxes you've paid for
Your plan is billed monthly, so when you cancel, the boxes for the month you've already paid for keep arriving until your billing date. After that no more boxes ship and you aren't charged again.

## Reactivating after cancelling
Cancelled accounts can be reactivated at any time from **Account → Subscription → Reactivate**. Reactivating costs a one-time $40 sign-up fee plus your plan's first month, unless you reactivate on the same plan before your billing date, which is free - see Pricing & Plans. Your saved preferences and delivery address are kept for 12 months after cancellation. After 12 months you may need to set up your plan again.

## Cancel vs. pause
If you're going away or just want a break, pause instead of cancelling — pausing keeps your plan and preferences and resumes automatically. Cancel if you don't plan to return soon.
