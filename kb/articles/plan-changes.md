# Changing Your Plan

## Plan sizes
FreshCrate offers 2, 3, and 4 meals per week. Your weekly price depends on the number of meals in your plan. You can see current pricing for each plan on the **Plans** page before you switch.

## Switching your plan size
Change your plan any time from **Account → Plan**. Switching the number of meals takes effect from your next unlocked box — the Wednesday 11:59pm weekly cutoff applies, so changes made after the cutoff start the following week.

## How billing changes when you switch
Switching is **prorated**: you're charged or credited the difference in the weekly rate for the weeks left until your billing date, and your monthly bill is at the new plan's price from then on. There's no fee to switch, and switching up or down doesn't reset your delivery day or preferences.

## Switching while paused
If you're paused, the switch happens straight away and your subscription stays paused. Nothing is prorated or charged at the time, and the new plan's weekly rate applies from when you resume. See the pausing article for details.

## Dietary preference vs. plan
Changing your dietary track (Standard, Gluten-Free, Vegetarian or Dairy-Free) is separate from changing your plan size. You can switch track without changing how many meals you get, and it's free - see the menu and dietary tracks article.
